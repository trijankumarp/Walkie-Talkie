How to connect Bluetooth
  1. Turn on Bluetooth on your phone or PC.
  2. Use Chrome or Edge (or Windows app).
  3. Tap Connect Bluetooth below and pick your device.
  4. Allow pairing. Status shows Connected or Not connected.
How to connect WiFi (team)
  1. Connect every team member to the same WiFi network.
  2. Open phone/PC WiFi settings and join that network.
  3. Tap Check WiFi below to see status (Windows app shows full list).
  4. Select the same team/channel in the menu for group talk.
